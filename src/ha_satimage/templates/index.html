Only enabled sources are fetched automatically. The source a region uses is selected with the region above. Downloads follow the source's scan cycle (Rapid Scan 5 min, MTG FCI 10 min, 0° 15 min) as soon as the new scan is available; afterwards the regions using the source are rendered. Archive of all sources

Downloads every enabled source in its own cycle (MSG SEVIRI Rapid Scan 5 min, 0° 15 min, MTG FCI 10 min) and renders every region in all image types of that source right away. The results are stored as PNG and JPEG, so the “🗄 Archive” button at a region browses them as fast as the normal viewer – nothing is rendered while browsing.

Clear archive deletes all archived images and FCI raw data. Normal image history is kept. Only scans newer than the clearing time will be archived; new scans can still fail if their rendering needs too much memory.

MTG FCI raw data archive

If an MTG FCI source is active, only the stripes (“chunks”, 1 = south … 40 = north) needed by the range below and all regions are downloaded per scan (Europe ≈ 32–40, ~180 MB per scan) and kept here. With “Download all sources” every region and image type is rendered from them right after the download; the archive viewer only shows these ready-made images.
